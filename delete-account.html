Linxova - Account and Data Deletion

Developer: JCLabs

Linxova uses Google Play Games Services for player authentication and game-related features. Linxova does not provide a separate JCLabs/Linxova account created with a username and password.

How to request deletion

To request deletion of data associated with your use of Linxova, please contact JCLabs at:

Email: admin@jclistening.com

Use the subject "Linxova Data Deletion Request" and indicate that you are requesting deletion of data associated with Linxova.

Google Play Games data

Some information used by Google Play Games Services is managed by Google. You can manage your Google Play Games profile, privacy settings, and associated data through your Google account and Google Play Games settings.

What data is deleted

Any Linxova user data controlled directly by JCLabs and associated with a valid deletion request will be deleted where applicable. Data controlled independently by Google must be managed through Google's services and account settings.

Data retention

JCLabs does not intentionally retain deleted Linxova user data after a valid deletion request has been completed, except where retention is required by applicable law or necessary for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Contact

For questions about privacy or data deletion related to Linxova, contact: admin@jclistening.com
